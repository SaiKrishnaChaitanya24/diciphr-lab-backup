Bridget Annavazala
Research Program Manager
Bridget is a Research Program Manager in the Department of Radiology. She has a masters in Experimental Psychology. She has spent the last 5 years as a Clinical Research Program Manager at Penn.

Bridget has overseen the clinical research operations for studies of Castleman disease and related cytokine driven disorders. Her work has focused on curation, collection, and analysis of longitudinal clinical data and biospecimens from patients with rare inflammatory diseases. She has supported multicenter observational studies and clinical trials. Her interests include clinical data management, biobanking, and patient-centered research.
