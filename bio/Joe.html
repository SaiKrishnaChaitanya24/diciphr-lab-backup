Joseph Opdenaker
Postdoctoral Researcher
I am a postdoctoral researcher focused on developing EEG capabilities for applications in traumatic brain injury and anomalous health incidents. I completed my PhD in Experimental Psychology with a specialization in cognitive neuroscience at Texas Tech University under Dr. Miranda Scolari and Dr. Jarrod Blinch, where I investigated underlying mechanisms of attentional and motor control using eye-tracking (e.g., pupillometry and microsaccade analysis), EEG (e.g., ERP and SSVEP analysis), and motion capture (e.g., kinematic profiling and trajectory analysis) methodologies.
  • B.A. Psychology, West Chester University of Pennsylvania
  • M.S. Health Psychology: Neuropsychology, The University of the Sciences in Philadelphia
  • Ph.D. Experimental Psychology: Cognition and Cognitive Neuroscience, Texas Tech University
